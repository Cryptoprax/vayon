# Premium Pricing Experience

## Commercial strategy

The public pricing experience positions VAYON as one AI Business Operating System rather than a collection of point features. It leads with business value, shows the platform products and outcomes, explains software consolidation, then presents commercial packages, governance, ROI context and enterprise engagement paths.

The page is a sales presentation layer only. The existing Subscription Engine, entitlement catalog, Stripe lifecycle and checkout services remain the authorities for purchasable products, billing state, quotas and access. Business Plus is presented as a sales-assisted package and therefore routes to Contact Sales; it does not create a new billing or entitlement plan.

## Pricing philosophy

- Starter supports founders launching with AI.
- Professional is the recommended growth package and is visually identified as Most Popular.
- Business supports established multi-function organizations.
- Business Plus supports sales-assisted multi-department and multi-location packaging.
- Enterprise supports negotiated deployment, security, integration, enablement and SLA requirements.

Monthly prices remain visible by default. The annual control provides a clearly labeled estimated annual-commitment discount without changing checkout logic. Localized monetary display reuses the shared public-site currency system.

## Business outcomes

The experience connects products to outcomes: launching a business, adding governed AI capacity, automating marketing, producing proposals and pitch decks, creating campaigns and websites, generating visual media, managing customers and operating the company from one workspace.

The comparison narrative deliberately discusses capabilities—not competitor brands or competitor pricing. ROI language is presented as an evaluation framework and does not fabricate customer savings.

## Enterprise positioning

Enterprise buyers receive a dedicated path covering custom AI, dedicated infrastructure, SSO, private cloud, custom integrations, a dedicated success manager, training, priority support and enterprise SLA options. The comparison matrix also exposes scale, governance, audit and administrative capabilities across all five commercial packages.

## Analytics foundation

The page declares commercial measurement points for plan popularity, upgrade funnel, conversion funnel, trial conversion and MRR/ARR projections. These foundations are intended to reuse existing conversion, subscription and Founder Dashboard evidence. No production values are synthesized in the UI.

## Future AI Credits roadmap

Future packaging can expose provider-neutral AI credits through the existing entitlement and quota architecture. A safe rollout should define capability-weighted consumption, transparent usage history, threshold notifications, overage policy, plan allowances and enterprise pools before displaying credits commercially. Pricing UI must continue to consume the central entitlement authority rather than embedding quota rules.

## Experience safeguards

The implementation uses VAYON design-system tokens and existing components, preserves public currency localization, provides semantic headings and table structure, supports keyboard-operable controls, and uses responsive grids and horizontal comparison-table scrolling. No authentication, billing, provider, runtime, database, migration or production configuration was changed.
