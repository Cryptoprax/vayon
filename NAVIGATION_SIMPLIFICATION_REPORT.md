## Remaining friction

- Large Marketing and Settings contexts still need broker usability testing; do not delete specialist tools based on assumed low use.
- Creative and AI access restrictions can hide advertised capabilities. Changing their policy is not part of this phase.
- Collapsed navigation uses tooltips/accessibility labels; test discovery with real users before adding another navigation surface.
- Internal and compatibility routes are not proven unused. Retain until usage and incoming links are verified.
