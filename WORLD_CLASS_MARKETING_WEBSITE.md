# World-Class Marketing Website

## Website architecture

The public experience continues to use the existing marketing route group, shared `MarketingShell`, VDS components, consent-aware analytics and public currency provider. Shared commercial catalogs now generate buyer-segment solutions, industry pages and flagship product pages with canonical metadata and Open Graph coverage. No application, authentication, billing, runtime, provider or database architecture was changed.

The public navigation follows the commercial journey: Home, Platform, Solutions, Industries, Pricing, Resources, Company and Contact, followed by Sign In and Start Free Trial. `/product` remains the public Platform destination because `/platform` is the protected administration namespace; this preserves security and avoids a route collision.

## SEO strategy

Every new public route defines a descriptive title, description, canonical URL and Open Graph metadata. Dynamic solution, industry and product routes expose static parameters and are included in the sitemap. Existing structured data, robots policy, blog metadata, documentation metadata and performance-oriented static generation remain in place.

## Legal coverage

The Legal Center covers Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy, Cookie Policy, Acceptable Use Policy, AI Usage Policy, Data Processing Addendum, Subprocessor List, Copyright Policy and Trademark Policy. The shared legal template includes version, effective date, table of contents, numbered sections and a legal contact path. Legal text is launch-ready information architecture and should receive qualified legal review before commercial execution.

## Trust strategy

Security content explicitly addresses authentication, encryption, RBAC, tenant isolation, backups, audit logs, AI safety, compliance roadmap and responsible disclosure. The Trust Center remains the assurance entry point. The public status page distinguishes operationally observed application availability from unknown provider/runtime state and does not fabricate uptime or history.

The contact experience exposes Sales, Support, Enterprise, Partnerships and General Enquiries paths alongside the required public mailbox placeholders and business hours. Published addresses remain explicitly unverified until mailbox configuration is operationally confirmed.

## Conversion strategy

The homepage preserves its premium interactive platform overview, trust proof, ROI, testimonials placeholders, Pricing experience and final CTA while broadening the hero to the AI Business Operating System position. Catalog pages consistently offer Start Free Trial and Book a Demo. Pricing continues to use Sprint 140 without changing checkout or entitlements.

## Commercial readiness

Paddle-review surfaces are discoverable: homepage, Platform, Pricing, Contact, Help, Terms, Privacy, Refund and Security. Resources include Blog, Documentation, Help Center, Release Notes, Status and a clearly labeled API Coming Soon page. Company coverage includes Mission, Vision, leadership placeholder, Careers, Partners, Press Kit and Brand Assets.

Founder commercial analytics declare website traffic, pricing conversion and trial conversion measurement points. Values remain unavailable until backed by existing production evidence; no metrics are synthesized.

Operational sign-off still requires legal review, mailbox provisioning, verified production status collection, payment-provider approval and approved customer testimonials.
